# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in **Telbot** a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, welcoming, and constructive in issues, pull requests, and community chats.
- Focus on clear technical feedback and user safety.
- Never submit secrets, tokens, or malicious code.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing others' private information or attempting to abuse public bot deployments.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or close abusive comments, issues, or pull requests and ban repeat offenders from project spaces.
